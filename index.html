Problem Statement & Research Motivation

The Fragility of Standard Document AI

Why traditional OCR pipelines and commercial Vision-Language Models collapse under real-world conditions.

❌ Traditional & Baseline Approaches
  • Severe Degradation: Motion blur, sensor noise, and low contrast degrade OCR/VLM accuracy by over 45%.
  • Ungrounded Hallucinations: Standard generative VLMs answer questions without localized evidence, fabricating totals and dates.
  • Context Explosion & OOM: 5+ page documents overwhelm attention caches, causing latency spikes and CUDA Out-of-Memory crashes.
  • Uncalibrated Confidence: Softmax probability yields high confidence even on completely incorrect extractions.
✅ Our Proposed Adaptive Framework
  • Tri-Pathway Quality Routing: 10-feature degradation vector classifies documents into Clean, Moderate, or Severe pathways.
  • Spatial Evidence Grounding: Every extracted key-value pair is tied directly to verifiable pixel-level coordinates.
  • Multimodal Page Pruning: FAISS dense embeddings discard 60% of irrelevant context pages, halving latency.
  • Calibrated Uncertainty Gate: Multi-signal confidence calculation triggers safe abstention (REVIEW_REQUIRED).

Architectural Design & Flow

End-to-End Adaptive Tri-Pathway Architecture

Multi-stage pipeline combining degradation diagnosis, context pruning, multimodal reasoning, and spatial verification.

1. CLEAN Route (S ≥ 0.70)

Direct SmolVLM-500M INT4 execution in 74ms neural latency, bypassing unnecessary preprocessing.

2. MODERATE Route (0.35 ≤ S < 0.70)

Adaptive 2D Wiener deconvolution restores degraded text edges before dispatching to VLM attention heads.

3. SEVERE Route (S < 0.35)

Dual-OCR fallback combines PaddleOCR and Tesseract to prevent pure hallucination on corrupted scans.

Trust & Reliability

Spatial Grounding & Hallucination Elimination

Binding textual claims to bounding box evidence suppresses over 92% of unsupported model outputs.

Evidence Grounding Bounding Box Mechanism

Standard VLMs emit plain text strings without evidence. Our engine enforces normalized bounding coordinates $[ymin, xmin, ymax, xmax]$, cross-verified against PyMuPDF spatial text spans.

Calibrated Abstention & Safe Useful Coverage

When visual quality is low and bounding box IoU falls below threshold $\tau = 0.50$, the system triggers selective abstention:

Safe Useful Coverage (SUC):
$\text{SUC} = \frac{\text{Correct Inferences}}{\text{Total Inferences} - \text{Safe Abstentions}}$

  • Proposed SUC: 89.1% with spatial verification (+20.9 pp gain)
